Ordinary Level (S1-S4)

Our O-Level program follows the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) curriculum, preparing students for the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE). We balance academic rigor with spiritual and character development, with structured lessons and practical learning.

Core Subjects

Mathematics

Covers numbers, algebra, geometry, and problem-solving. Builds logical thinking and analytical skills. Our school ensures strong mastery through regular practice and teacher-guided revision.

Physics

Studies matter, energy, forces, motion, electricity, and waves. Our lessons focus on experiments and real-life applications to make concepts clear.

Chemistry

Explores substances, reactions, acids, bases, and laboratory skills. Students learn through safe, hands-on experiments and structured theory notes.

Biology

The science of living things — plants, animals, health, and the human body. Our school strengthens understanding through practical observations and fieldwork.

English Language

Covers grammar, comprehension, literature basics, writing, and communication skills. We emphasize speaking, writing quality, and comprehension excellence.

Geography

Study of the earth, environment, weather, population, and map work. Our school combines field studies and real-world examples to deepen understanding.

History

Covers world civilizations, African history, early kingdoms, colonialism, and modern governance. Students are guided to understand past events and connect them to modern society.

Optional Subjects

Information & Communication Technology (ICT)

Teaches computers, digital tools, coding basics, and technology skills. Our ICT teaching is practical, industry-focused, and future-oriented.

Kiswahili

Language used across East Africa. Teaches communication, grammar, and culture. Our lessons emphasize fluency and real conversational practice.

Luganda

Local language covering grammar, vocabulary, reading, and cultural understanding. Taught with a cultural approach to strengthen identity and communication.

Entrepreneurship

Teaches business skills, innovation, financial literacy, and problem-solving. We train learners to think creatively and develop real business ideas.

Food & Nutrition

Covers healthy eating, food preparation, cooking, hygiene, and diet planning. Lessons are practical and safely supervised in well-organized environments.

Technology & Design (T&D)

Teaches design skills, drawing, technical diagrams, craft work, and project building. Our practical approach helps learners build creativity and technical skill.

Agriculture

Covers farming, livestock, crops, soil science, and agribusiness basics. We integrate field practice and demonstrations for deeper learning.

Fine Art

Drawing, painting, sculpture, craft, design, and creative expression. Students receive structured guidance to grow their artistic skills.

Literature in English

Studies novels, poetry, plays, themes, characters, and critical analysis. Teaching promotes strong reading, interpretation, and expressive skills.

Our Teaching Approach

“Our school is committed to delivering each subject with care, clarity, and practical learning. Teachers follow structured lesson plans, provide personalized support, and create an environment where every learner can understand, grow, and excel.”

Advanced Level (S5-S6)

Our A-Level program prepares students for UACE examinations. Students specialize in combinations designed to prepare them for university and careers.

Science (PCM)

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology — for medicine, engineering and sciences.

Arts (HEG)

History, Economics, Divinity/Geography — ideal for law, social sciences and humanities.

Commerce (BAM/ICT)

Business, Accounts, Mathematics/ICT — prepares students for business and finance careers.

Many More Combinations

We offer a wide range of subject combinations to suit different interests and career paths. Our academic advisors help students choose the best fit for their goals.

Vocational Programs

Practical skills training alongside academic education to prepare students for employment and technical further education.

Computer Studies

Hardware, software, networking and programming for IT careers.

Building & Construction

Carpentry, masonry, plumbing and electrical skills.

Tailoring & Fashion

Garment construction, design and entrepreneurship.
