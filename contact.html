Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements?

We accept students based on their PLE results, interview performance, and available space. Minimum pass mark is aggregate 28.

Learn more

What are the school fees?

Our fees structure varies by class and boarding status. We offer flexible payment plans and some scholarships for exceptional students.

View fees structure

Do you offer boarding facilities?

Yes, we provide separate boarding facilities for boys and girls with 24/7 supervision and modern amenities.

View facilities

When does the term start?

Our academic calendar follows the Ministry of Education schedule. Term dates are communicated to parents in advance.

View calendar

What curriculum do you follow?

We follow the Uganda National Curriculum with additional emphasis on practical skills and Christian values.

View curriculum

When can parents visit?

Parents can visit during designated visiting days and by appointment for special circumstances.
